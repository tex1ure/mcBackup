# Privacy Policy

**mcBackup** respects your privacy.

## Data Collection

- **No Telemetry or Analytics:** mcBackup does **not** collect, track, or transmit any usage data, crash reports, or personal information to any third-party servers.
- **Google Drive Access:** When you connect Google Drive via `rclone`, your OAuth token is stored locally on your device by `rclone` and is used solely to upload and prune your Minecraft world `.zip` backups in your own Google Drive (`MinecraftBackups/`).
- **Local Files:** World paths, backup timestamps, and logs are stored locally in `data/config.json`, `data/state.json`, and `data/logs/backup.log` on your machine.
